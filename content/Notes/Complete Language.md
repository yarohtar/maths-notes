Let $C$ be a class of languages and $L\in C$ 
We say $L$ is $C$-Complete if it is $C$-[[Notes/Hard Language]].

### Examples
[[Notes/Computability Hierarchy]]
- Any [[Notes/Computable]] language $\emptyset \neq L\neq \mathbb{B}$ is $\Delta_{1}$-complete 
- [[Notes/The Halting Problem]] $K$ is $\Sigma_{1}$-complete
