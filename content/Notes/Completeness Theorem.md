[[Notes/Syntactic Entailment (Propositional Logic)]] 
and 
[[Notes/Semantic Entailment (Propositional Logic)]] 
are equivalent.
In other words:
Let $S\subseteq L$, $t\in L$. Then $S\models t$ if and only if $S\vdash t$.

[[Notes/Soundness Theorem (Propositional Logic)]] 
and [[Notes/Adequacy Theorem (Propositional Logic)]].
