We study how the [[Notes/Information Rate]] behaves for [[Notes/Size of Largest Code]] as $n\to \infty$.
$$
\alpha(\delta)=\limsup \frac{1}{n}\log A(n,n\delta)
$$
### Proposition
$$
1-H(\delta)\leq \alpha(\delta)\leq 1-H\left( \frac{\delta}{2} \right)
$$
where $H$ is [[Notes/Mathematical Entropy]]
