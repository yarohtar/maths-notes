Let $X$ be a complex [[Notes/Banach Space]], $T\in L(X)$, $p$ a nonconstant complex polynomial. 
Then [[Notes/Spectrum]] of $p(T)$ satisfies:
$$
\sigma(p(T))=\{ p(\lambda):\lambda \in \sigma(T) \}
$$
### Corollary
Let $X$ be a complex [[Notes/Banach Space]], $T\in L(X)$.
Then [[Notes/Spectral Value]] satisfies $r(T)\leq \inf_{n\geq 1}\lVert T^{n} \rVert^{1/n}$
