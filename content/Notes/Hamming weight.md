The weight of $x\in \mathbb{F}_{2}^{n}$ is $w(x)=d(x,0)$
The weight $w(C)$ of a [[Notes/Linear Code]] $C$ is the minimum weight of a nonzero codeword.

### Lemma
The [[Notes/Minimum distance of a code]] [[Notes/Linear Code]] $C$ is the minimum weight of a nonzero codeword.

#### Proof
Let $x,y\in C$
$$
d(x,y)=d(x-y,0)=d(x+y,0)=w(x+y)
$$
Note that $x$ and $y$ are distinct so $x+y\neq 0$
