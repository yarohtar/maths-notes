## Postulate I
States are vectors $\ket{\psi}$ in a [[Notes/Hilbert Space\|Hilbert space]] $\mathcal{H}$.
## Postulate II
[[Notes/Observables]] are [[Notes/Linear Operator\|linear]] [[Notes/Adjoint\|Hermetian]] operators $Q:\mathcal{H}\to \mathcal{H}$.

## Postulate III
The dynamical evolution of a quantum system is governed by
$$
i\hbar \frac{ \partial  }{ \partial t } \ket{\psi} =H\ket{\psi} 
$$
The time dependent Schrödinger equation (TDSE), for some Hamiltonian operator $H$. $H$ does NOT include time
