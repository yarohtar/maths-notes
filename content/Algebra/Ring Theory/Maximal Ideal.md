An [[Algebra/Ring Theory/Ideal]] $I\triangleleft R$ is maximal if there is no $J\triangleleft R$ s.t. $I\subsetneq J$ and $J\neq R$. 

### Proposition
Let $I\triangleleft R$ be a proper [[Algebra/Ring Theory/Ideal]].
Then $R/I$ is a [[Algebra/Field]] iff $I$ is maximal.
#### Proof
$R/I$ is a field [[Algebra/Ring Theory/Ideal#Lemma\|iff]] $I/I$ and $R/I$ are the only ideals of $R/I$, 
which happens iff $I$ and $R$ are the only ideals in $R$ containing $I$ 
i.e. $I$ is maximal.
### Proposition
Every maximal ideal is a [[Algebra/Ring Theory/Prime Ideal]].
