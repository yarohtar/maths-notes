Let $H$ be [[Algebra/Group Theory/Normal]] in a [[Algebra/Group Theory/Group]] $G$.
We define the quotient group $G / H$ with elements:
$$
G / H = \{ gH : g \in G \}
$$
i.e. the set of [[Algebra/Group Theory/Coset\|cosets]]
and operation $(gH)\cdot(hH)=(gh)H$
### Proposition
Operation above is well defined and $G /H$ is a [[Algebra/Group Theory/Group]] under it.
