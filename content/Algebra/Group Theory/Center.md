Let $G$ be a [[Algebra/Group Theory/Group]].
The center of $G$ is the intersection of all [[Algebra/Group Theory/Centralizer\|centralizers]]:
$$
Z(G)= \bigcap_{g\in G} C_{G}(g)
$$
It is the set of elements that commute with everything.
### Proposition
The center $Z(G)$ is [[Algebra/Group Theory/Normal]] [[Algebra/Group Theory/Subgroup]] of a [[Algebra/Group Theory/Group]] $G$.
