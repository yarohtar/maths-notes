A [[Algebra/Group Theory/Group]] is simple if it has no proper [[Algebra/Group Theory/Normal]] [[Algebra/Group Theory/Subgroup\|subgroups]].

[[Algebra/Group Theory/Simple Group Decomposition]]
### Theorem
[[Algebra/Group Theory/Abelian]] [[Algebra/Group Theory/Group]] is simple if and only if it is isomorphic to $C_p$ for some prime $p$.
