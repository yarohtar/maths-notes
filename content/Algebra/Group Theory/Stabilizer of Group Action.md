Suppose we have a [[Algebra/Group Theory/Group action]] of [[Algebra/Group Theory/Group]] $G$ on a set $X$
Let $\phi$ be a group [[Algebra/Group Theory/Homomorphism]] $G\to Sym_{X}$ 
corresponding to this action

Then the stabilizer of the group action is:
$$
\ker \phi= \bigcap_{x\in X} G_{x}
$$
where $G_{x}$ is [[Algebra/Group Theory/Stabilizer]] of $x$
