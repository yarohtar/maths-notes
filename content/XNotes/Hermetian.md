Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a [[XNotes/Hilbert Space]] and $T\in L(\mathcal{H})$ be a [[XNotes/Linear Operator]]. 
Then $T$ is Hermetian or self-[[Analysis/Adjoint]] if $T^{*}=T$
i.e. if $(Tx,y)=(x,Ty)$

### Proposition
For a complex [[XNotes/Hilbert Space]] $\mathcal{H}$ and a [[XNotes/Linear Operator]] $T\in L(\mathcal{H})$, 
there exist unique $T_{1},T_{2}\in L(\mathcal{H})$ Hermetian with $T=T_{1}+iT_{2}$.
#### Proof
$T_{1}=\frac{1}{2}(T+T^{*})$
$T_{2}=\frac{1}{2}(T^{*}-T)$
