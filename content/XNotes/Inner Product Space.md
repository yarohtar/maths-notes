Given a real or complex vector space $X$, and an [[XNotes/Inner Product]] $(,)$ on $X$ we say that $(X,(,))$ is an inner product space.
[[XNotes/Orthonormal Sequence]]
[[XNotes/Orthonormal Basis]]
### Theorem
For all $x,y\in X$ we have:
1. $\lvert (x,y) \rvert\leq \lVert x \rVert\cdot \lVert y \rVert$
2. $\lVert x+y \rVert\leq \lVert x \rVert+\lVert y \rVert$
