A covering space of a space $X$ is a pair $(\tilde{X}, p)$ where
- $\tilde{X}$ is a space
- $p: \tilde{X}\to X$ is a map such that 
  for all $x \in X$ there is a [[XNotes/Neighbourhood\|nbd]] $U$ of $x$ such that $p ^{-1}(U)=\sqcup_{\alpha\in A}V_{\alpha}$, 
  where $V_{\alpha}\subset \tilde X$ open, and $p|_{V_{\alpha}}:V_{\alpha}\to U$ is a [[XNotes/Homeomorphism]]
$p$ is called a covering map. 
Open sets $U$ as above are called evenly covered.
