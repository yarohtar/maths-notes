Let $X$ be an [[XNotes/Inner Product Space]]. A sequence $(x_{n})$ is an orthonormal basis if:
1. it is an [[XNotes/Orthonormal Sequence]]
2. $\langle x_{n}:n=1,2,\dots \rangle$ is dense in $X$ 

We use the same terminology for a finite sequence
[[XNotes/Gram-Schmidt process]] applies

### Corollary
Let $X$ be a [[XNotes/Separable Space]] [[XNotes/Inner Product Space]]. Then $X$ has an orthonormal basis.

### Corollary
Let $X$ be an $n$-dimensional inner product space. Then $X$ is [[XNotes/Isometric Isomorphism]] to $l_{2}^{n}$.
