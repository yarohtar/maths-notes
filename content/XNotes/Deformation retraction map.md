### Definition
Let $X$ be space and $A\subset X$. A deformation retraction of $X$ to $A$ is a [[XNotes/Retraction map\|retraction]] $r$ where $i\circ r\simeq id_{X}$. ($i:A\to X$, $i(a)=a$)

Note that this implies that $A\simeq X$ (because $r\circ i\simeq id_{A}$ is trivial)
(see [[XNotes/Homotopy#Definition (homotopy-equivalence)\|homotopy equivalence]])
This is not true in [[XNotes/Retraction map\|retraction]], because we can just map $X$ to a single point.
### Definition
If additionally $i\circ r\simeq id_{X}$ relative to $A$, then $r$ is a strong deformation retraction.
