Let $(E,\mathcal{E},\mu)$ be a [[Analysis/Measure/Measure Space]]
Suppose there exists a sequence $(E_{n})$ in $\mathcal{E}$ with
$$
\bigcup_{n} E_{n} = E
$$
and for all $n\in \mathbb{N}$:
$$
\mu(E_{n})<\infty
$$
Then $\mu$ is a $\sigma$-finite measure.

A [[Analysis/Measure/Finite Measure]] is always $\sigma$-finite.
