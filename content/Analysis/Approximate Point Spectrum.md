Write $\sigma_{ap}(T)=\{ \lambda \in \mathbb{C} : \lambda\text{ is approximate eigenvalue of }T\}$
[[Analysis/Approximate Eigenvalue]]

### Theorem
Let $X$ be a complex [[Analysis/Banach Space]] and $T\in L(X)$
Then the border of [[XNotes/Spectrum]] satisfies:
$$
\partial \sigma(T)\subseteq \sigma_{ap}(T)
$$
