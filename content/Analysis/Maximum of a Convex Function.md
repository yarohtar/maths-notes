Let $S$ be a [[Topology/Compact]] [[Algebra/Vector Spaces/Convex Set]].
Let $f:S\to \mathbb{R}$ be a [[Topology/Continuous]] [[Analysis/Convex Function]].
Then $f$ achieves its maximum at an [[Analysis/Extreme Point]] of $S$.

### Proof
Let $m=\max(f(S))$.
It exists as $S$ is [[Topology/Compact]] and $f$ is [[Topology/Continuous]]
Now set
$$
U=\{ x\in S : f(x) < m\}
$$
As $f$ is [[Topology/Continuous]], $U$ is open (in $S$)
As $f$ is a [[Analysis/Convex Function]], $U$ is a [[Algebra/Vector Spaces/Convex Set]].
Suppose $U$ contains all [[Analysis/Extreme Point]]s of $S$
