Let $\mathcal{H}$ be [[Separable]] [[Hilbert Space]]
A density operator is a [[Linear Operator]] $\rho \in \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})$
which is positive semi-[[Definite]] and has [[Trace]] one:

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(\rho)=1
$$
