Let $\mathcal{H}$ be [[Topology/Separable]] [[Analysis/Hilbert Space/Hilbert Space]]
A density operator is a [[Analysis/Linear Operator]] $\rho \in \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})$ 
which is positive semi-[[Analysis/Definite]] and has [[Analysis/Trace]] one:
$$
\operatorname{Tr}(\rho)=1
$$
