A quantum channel is a [[Quantum/Information/Completely Positive]], [[Analysis/Trace]] preserving, [[Analysis/Linear Operator]]:
$$
T:\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{H}_{1})\to \mathcal{S}(\mathcal{H}_{2})
$$
where $\mathcal{S}$ is the space of [[Quantum/Density Operator\|Quantum State]]s

### Theorem
Any quantum channel is a combination of: 
- [[Analysis/Unitary]] evolution $\rho \to U^{\dagger}\rho U$
- Adding an ancilla $\rho \to \rho \otimes \rho_{E}$
- [[Analysis/Partial Trace]]
