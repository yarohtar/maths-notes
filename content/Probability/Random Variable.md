Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})$ be a [[Probability/Probability Space]].
Let $(E,\mathcal{E})$ be a [[Analysis/Measure/Measurable Space]].
A random variable is a [[Analysis/Measure/Measurable Function]] $X:\Omega \to E$.
We write:
$$
\mathbb{P}(X\in S) := \mathbb{P}(\{ \omega \in \Omega : X(\omega)\in S \})
$$
for any [[Probability/Measurable Set]] $S\subseteq E$.

Usually we have $E\subseteq\mathbb{R}$ with the induced [[Analysis/Measure/Borel Sigma Algebra]]
