Let $A$ be a [[Combinatorics/Counting/Combinatorial Structure]].
We define 
$$
A'(U) = A(U\cup \{ * \})
$$
the derivative of $A$ (where $*\not\in U$)
Sometimes we may write $A^{+}$ instead of $A'$
### Lemma
The [[Combinatorics/Counting/Exponential Generating Function]] satisfies
$$
\tilde{f}_{A'}(x) = \tilde{f}_{A}'(x)
$$
