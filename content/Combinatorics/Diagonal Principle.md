Let $V$ be a [[Algebra/Vector Spaces/Vector Space]] over some [[Algebra/Field]] $F$, with [[Algebra/Vector Spaces/Dual Vector Space]] $V^{*}$
Let $v_{1},\dots,v_{m}\in V$ and $u_{1},\dots,u_{m}\in V^{*}$.
Suppose that 
$$
u_{i}(v_{j}) = f_{i} \delta_{ij}
$$
where $f_{i}\in F$ are nonzero.
Then $v_{1},\dots,v_{m}$ are linearly independent.
