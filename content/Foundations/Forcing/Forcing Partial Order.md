Let $(\mathbb{P},\leq)$ be a [[Foundations/Poset/Poset]] and $\mathbb{1}\in \mathbb{P}$ be the maximal element.
Then $(\mathbb{P},\leq,\mathbb{1})$ is called a forcing.

A subset $P\subseteq \mathbb{P}$ is said to be open if 
$$
(\forall p\in P)\,(\forall q\in \mathbb{P})\, q\leq p \implies q\in P
$$
We can check that this makes $\mathbb{P}$ a [[Topology/Topological space]].
This allows us to talk about [[Topology/Dense]] subsets of $\mathbb{P}$,
in particular defined in [[Foundations/Poset/Dense Below]].
