Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a family of [[Foundations/Poset/Dense Below\|Dense]] sets in a [[Foundations/Forcing/Forcing Partial Order]] $(\mathbb{P},\leq,\mathbb{1})$
Let $F$ be a [[Foundations/Poset/Filter]].
Then we say that $F$ is $\mathcal{D}$-generic if 
$$
(\forall D\in \mathcal{D})\, F\cap D \neq \varnothing
$$
Furthermore, if $M$ is a [[Foundations/Transitive Model]] of some $T\subseteq ZFC$
we say that $F$ is $\mathbb{P}$-generic over $M$ if 
$$
\mathcal{D}=\{ D\subseteq \mathbb{P} : D\in M \text{ and } D\text{ is dense} \}
$$
and $F$ is $\mathcal{D}$-generic.
### Theorem
If $\mathcal{D}$ is countable, then there is a $\mathcal{D}$-generic [[Foundations/Poset/Filter]].
#### Proof
Index $\mathcal{D}$ as 
$$
\mathcal{D}=\{ D_{i} : i\in \mathbb{N} \}
$$
Fix some $p_{0}\in \mathbb{P}$.
Now construct recursively $p_{i+1}$ such that 
$$
p_{i+1}\leq p_{i} \land p_{i+1} \in D_{i}
$$
(possible as $D_{i}$ is [[Topology/Dense]])
Then 
$$
B=\{ p_{i} : i\in \mathbb{N} \}
$$
is a [[Foundations/Poset/Filter Base]].
The [[Foundations/Poset/Filter]] generated by $B$ is $\mathcal{D}$-generic.
### Corollary
If $M$ is a countable [[Foundations/Transitive Model]] of some $T\subseteq ZFC$, 
and $(\mathbb{P},\leq,\mathbb{1})\in M$ is a [[Foundations/Forcing/Forcing Partial Order]],
then there is a $\mathbb{P}$-generic filter over $M$.
#### Proof
The set $\mathcal{D}=\{ D\subseteq \mathbb{P}: D\in M \text{ and }D\text{ is dense} \}$ is countable.
Therefore, there is a $\mathcal{D}$-generic filter (by previous theorem).
This is the $\mathbb{P}$-generic filter over $M$.
