A reflective subcategory of $\mathcal{C}$ 
is a [[Foundations/Category Theory/Full]] subcategory $\mathcal{D}$ for which the inclusion
$$
\mathcal{D}\to \mathcal{C}
$$
has a left [[Foundations/Category Theory/Adjunction\|Adjoint]].
Note that this [[Foundations/Category Theory/Adjunction]] is necessarily a [[Foundations/Category Theory/Reflection]], as inclusions are always [[Foundations/Category Theory/Faithfull]].
