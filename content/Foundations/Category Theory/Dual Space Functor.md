Dual space construction is a [[Foundations/Category Theory/Functor]] $\mathrm{Vect}_{K}^{op}\to \mathrm{Vect}_{K}$ (or [[Foundations/Category Theory/Contravariant]] functor)
it sends $V$ to $V^{*}$
and $V\xrightarrow{\theta}W$ to $W^{*}\xrightarrow{\theta ^{*}}V^{*}$
defined by composition with $\theta$
