Let $f:A\to B$ be a morphism in [[Foundations/Category Theory/Category]] $\mathcal{C}$
We say $f$ is a monomorphism if 
$$
fg=fh\implies g=h
$$
for all $C\xrightarrow{g,h}A$
We denote a monomorphism $f$ by $A\rightarrowtail^{f}B$

In [[Foundations/Category Theory/Category of Sets]], monomorphisms are injective.
