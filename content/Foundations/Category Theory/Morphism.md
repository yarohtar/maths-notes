A morphism of a [[Foundations/Category Theory/Category]] $\mathcal{C}$ is an element of the set $\operatorname{mor}\mathcal{C}$
Each morphism $f$ has two associated [[Foundations/Category Theory/Object]]s, 
namely $A=\operatorname{dom}f$ and $B=\operatorname{cod}f$ 
and we write 
$$
f:A\to B
$$
or
$$
A\xrightarrow{f}B
$$
