By a skeleton of $\mathcal{C}$, we mean the [[Foundations/Category Theory/Full Subcategory]] $\mathcal{C}'$ 
containing just one [[Foundations/Category Theory/Object]] for each [[Foundations/Category Theory/Isomorphism]] class.

A skeleton is always [[Foundations/Category Theory/Skeletal]].


### Example
The [[Foundations/Category Theory/Full Subcategory]] of $\mathrm{fdVect}_{K}$ with the spaces $K^{n}$ as [[Foundations/Category Theory/Object]]s
is a skeleton of $\mathrm{fdVect}_{K}$
