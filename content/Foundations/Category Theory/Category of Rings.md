Category of [[Algebra/Ring Theory/Ring\|Ring]]s and ring homomorphisms denoted by $\mathrm{Rng}$

In $\mathrm{Rng}$, [[Foundations/Category Theory/Monomorphism]]s are injective (use $\mathbb{Z}[X]$)
but $\mathbb{Z}\to \mathbb{Q}$ is an [[Foundations/Category Theory/Epimorphism]] as well as [[Foundations/Category Theory/Monomorphism]], 
so $\mathrm{Rng}$ is not [[Foundations/Category Theory/Balanced]].
