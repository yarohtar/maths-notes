The [[Foundations/Category Theory/Category]] of [[Topology/Topological space]]s and [[Topology/Continuous]] maps between them
denoted by $\mathrm{Top}$

Note that [[Foundations/Category Theory/Isomorphism]]s in $\mathrm{Top}$ are [[Topology/Homeomorphism]]s,
but there are [[Topology/Continuous]] bijections that are not [[Foundations/Category Theory/Isomorphic]]
and thus $\mathrm{Top}$ is not [[Foundations/Category Theory/Balanced]].
