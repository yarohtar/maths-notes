The fundamental groupoid $\prod(X)$ of a space $X$ is the [[Foundations/Category Theory/Category]]
with objects being the points of $X$
and morphisms $x\to y$ are [[Homotopy/Homotopy]] classes of paths from $x$ to $y$

This is a [[Foundations/Category Theory/Groupoid]] by a proof similar to [[Homotopy/Fundamental group]]
