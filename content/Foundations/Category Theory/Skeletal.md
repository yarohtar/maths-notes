We say a [[Foundations/Category Theory/Category]] $\mathcal{C}$ is skeletal if each [[Foundations/Category Theory/Isomorphism]] class in $\operatorname{ob}\mathcal{C}$ has just one member.
By a skeleton of $\mathcal{C}$, we mean the [[Foundations/Category Theory/Full Subcategory]] $\mathcal{C}'$ 
containing just one object for each isomorphism class.

E.g. [[Foundations/Category Theory/Category of Matrices]] $\mathrm{Mat}_{K}$ is skeletal
and the full subcategory of $\mathrm{fdVect}_{K}$ on the spaces $K^{n}$ 
is a skeleton of $\mathrm{fdVect}_{K}$
