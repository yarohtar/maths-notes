We say a [[Foundations/Category Theory/Category]] $\mathcal{C}$ is skeletal 
if each [[Foundations/Category Theory/Isomorphism]] class in $\operatorname{ob}\mathcal{C}$ has just one member.

[[Foundations/Category Theory/Skeleton]]

### Example
The [[Foundations/Category Theory/Category of Matrices]] $\mathrm{Mat}_{K}$ is skeletal.
