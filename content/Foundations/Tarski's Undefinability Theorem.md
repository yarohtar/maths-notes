Let $\mathcal{L}$ be a first-order [[Foundations/First Order Logic/Language]],
and let $M$ be an $\mathcal{L}$-[[Foundations/First Order Logic/Structure]] that can encode formulas in $\mathcal{L}$ 
(e.g. by Godel encodings $g(\phi)$)
Then there is no formula $\mathrm{True}$ such that 
$$
\mathrm{True}(g(\phi)) \iff M\models \phi
$$
