Let $S\subseteq L$ and $t\in L$. We say that $S$ entails $t$ if for any valuation $v$
$$
v(s)=1 \text{ for all } s \in S \implies v(t)=1
$$
We denote this by $S\models t$

Note: $S\models t$ iff $t$ is [[Foundations/Propositional Logic/True (Propositional Logic)]] 
in every [[Foundations/Propositional Logic/Model (Propositional Logic)]] of $S$.
