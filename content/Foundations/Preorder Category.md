Suppose [[Foundations/Category]] $\mathcal{C}$ is such that for any objects $A$ and $B$
there is at most one morphism $A\to B$
Then $\operatorname{mor}\mathcal{C}$ is a binary relation on $\operatorname{ob}\mathcal{C}$ which is reflective and transitive
and $\mathcal{C}$ is called a preorder.
A [[Foundations/Partial Order\|partially ordered set]] is a [[Foundations/Small Category\|small]] preorder whose only isomorphisms are identities
