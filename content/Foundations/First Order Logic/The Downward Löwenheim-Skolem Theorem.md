Let $S$ be a [[Foundations/First Order Logic/Theory]] in a countable language (i.e. $\Omega \cup \Pi$ is countable)
If $S$ has a [[Foundations/First Order Logic/Model (First-order Logic)]], then $S$ has a countable model.
#### Proof
By the [[Foundations/First Order Logic/Soundness Theorem (First-order Logic)]], $S$ is consistent.
Then the model constructed in the proof of [[Foundations/First Order Logic/Model Existence Lemma (First-order Logic)]] is countable.
