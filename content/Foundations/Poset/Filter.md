Let $(P,\leq)$ be a [[Foundations/Poset/Poset]].
A [[Foundations/Poset/Filter Base]] $F\subseteq P$ is called a filter if it is upwards closed.
### Lemma
Let $B$ be a [[Foundations/Poset/Filter Base]].
Then the [[Foundations/Poset/Upward Closure]] of $B$:
$$
\{ p\in P: (\exists q\in B)\,q\leq p \}
$$
is a [[Foundations/Poset/Filter]] - the filter generated by $B$.
